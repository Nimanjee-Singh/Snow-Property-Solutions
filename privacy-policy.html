Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Draft for review. This document is a starting template. Please have it reviewed and confirmed by the business owner (and, where appropriate, a legal adviser) before relying on it.

Who we are

Snow Property Solutions provides painting and decorating services in London and surrounding areas. For any privacy questions, contact us at nimanjee1@gmail.com or on 07407 072094.

Information we collect

When you use our quote calculator or contact form we collect the information you choose to give us, which may include your name, email address, phone number, property postcode, preferred contact method, project details, measurements and any photographs you upload.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare an estimate or quotation.
  • To contact you about your project using your preferred method.
  • To carry out and manage work you ask us to do.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Lawful basis

We process your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and where necessary for our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and running our business.

Photographs

Photos you upload are used only to understand your project. Images are resized and location data embedded in the file is removed in your browser before upload.

How your information is sent and stored

Enquiries submitted through this website are delivered to our business email through a third-party form-processing service. We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting work, and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record your IP address as part of delivering the files.
