About Snow Property Solutions

Painting and decorating for homes, landlords and businesses across London — built on preparation, care and a finish we are proud to put our name to.

Our story

Exceptional decorating starts long before the paint

At Snow Property Solutions, we believe that exceptional decorating starts with careful preparation, quality workmanship and attention to every detail.

We provide painting and decorating services for residential and commercial properties, helping customers refresh, improve and transform their spaces.

From smaller decorating projects to larger property improvements, our focus is on delivering a professional service and a finish that meets the needs of each customer.

Our approach

Preparation and finishing

Most problems with a painted finish trace back to the surface underneath. Before we decorate, we check for cracks, flaking paint, stains and uneven areas, then fill, sand and prime as needed so the new finish bonds properly and looks even.

Finishing is where the care shows: crisp lines where walls meet ceilings, clean edges around switches and frames, and consistent sheen across every surface. We check our work in different light before calling a room finished.
