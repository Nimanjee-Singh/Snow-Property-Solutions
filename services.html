Our services

Professional Painting & Decorating Services

From a single room to a full commercial refurbishment, each of our services follows a clear process — prepare properly, protect the property and finish with care.

Get an Instant Estimate
01 · Walls, ceilings, woodwork and doors

Interior Painting

Interior painting for living spaces, bedrooms, hallways and rental properties. We prepare every surface properly, protect your furnishings and apply finishes that suit how each room is used — from washable matt in busy hallways to durable satin on woodwork.

Main benefits

  • A refreshed, even finish throughout
  • Finishes suited to each room’s use
  • Minimal disruption to daily life

Key work included

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Skirting, doors and frames
  • Filling, sanding and priming
  • Colour and sheen guidance

02 · Masonry, windows, fascias and doors

Exterior Painting

Exterior painting that improves the look of your property and helps protect exposed surfaces. Walls, masonry, woodwork, fascias and fences are cleaned and prepared, then coated with exterior products suited to the surface.

Main benefits

  • Refreshed kerb appeal
  • Coatings suited to exterior exposure
  • Protection for exposed surfaces

Key work included

  • Masonry and rendered walls
  • Doors and windows
  • Fascias and soffits
  • Cleaning and surface preparation

03 · Feature walls and full rooms

Wallpapering

Wallpaper hanging for feature walls and full rooms, including removal of old paper and preparation of the walls beneath. Patterns are set out and matched carefully so the finished wall looks continuous.

Main benefits

  • Character and texture in any room
  • Seamless pattern alignment
  • Clean edges and corners

Key work included

  • Feature walls and full rooms
  • Removal of old wallpaper
  • Wall preparation and lining
  • Pattern matching

04 · Cracks, skimming and surface repair

Plastering & Repairs

Plastering and wall repairs that give your decoration a sound, smooth base. Cracks, dents and damaged areas are repaired, and surfaces are filled, skimmed or smoothed as needed before painting.

Main benefits

  • Smooth surfaces ready for decoration
  • Defects repaired, not painted over
  • A better final paint finish

Key work included

  • Crack and hole repairs
  • Ceiling repairs
  • Skimming and patching
  • Filling and smoothing

Commercial Decorating

Decorating for offices, retail premises, communal areas and managed properties. We agree working arrangements that suit your business and deliver a consistent, professional finish across larger spaces.

Main benefits

  • A professional environment for staff and customers
  • Work planned around your operations
  • Consistent finish across larger areas

Key work included

  • Offices and retail premises
  • Communal areas
  • Rental and managed properties
  • Refurbishment decorating
