01General

We provide interior painting, exterior painting, wallpapering, plastering and repairs and commercial decorating.

We work across London and surrounding areas. If you’re unsure whether we cover your location, send us your postcode and we’ll confirm. See Areas We Cover.

Yes. We decorate homes and rental properties as well as offices, retail premises, communal areas and other commercial spaces.

02Pricing and quotations

The cost depends on the type of work, the condition of the surfaces, how much preparation is needed, access, and the finishes chosen. Our instant estimate calculator uses our current rates to give an initial price range, and we confirm the final price in a personalised quotation.

Yes. Quotations are free and there is no obligation to go ahead.

You choose a service, enter the surface area in square metres (or room dimensions, and we calculate the wall area), select the surface condition and any additional work, and upload photos if you have them. The calculator then shows an initial estimated range. Where an item can’t be estimated online, we’ll price it in your personalised quotation.

Whether materials are included will be set out clearly in your quotation, so you know exactly what you’re paying for. If you’re supplying your own wallpaper or paint, let us know.

Yes. Every estimate is followed by a personalised quotation based on your requirements and, where needed, a visit to see the property.
