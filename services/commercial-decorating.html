Tailored to your property

Working around your business

Every commercial property operates differently. A retail unit may need work completed before opening; an occupied office may need noisy or disruptive tasks scheduled carefully; a block of flats needs communal areas kept safe and accessible.

We discuss these requirements at the start and plan the project accordingly — agreeing phasing, access, working hours and how areas will be protected and kept tidy.

We'll discuss

  • Access arrangements and working hours
  • Phasing to keep areas in use
  • Durable finishes for high-traffic areas
  • Site contacts and communication

Our process

Planned for minimal disruption

Clear planning up front means fewer surprises once work begins.

  1. Site assessment

    We review the areas, surfaces, access and any operational constraints.

  2. Plan and agree

    A schedule and working arrangements agreed with you, with a clear quotation.

  3. Prepare and decorate

    Areas protected and decorated in agreed phases, kept tidy and safe throughout.

  4. Handover

    A walk-through of completed areas and touch-ups before sign-off.
