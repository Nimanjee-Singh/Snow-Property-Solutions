Exterior painting

First impressions, built to last

The outside of a property takes the weather every day. A good exterior finish needs to look smart and stand up to it.

That starts with what's beneath the paint. We clean down surfaces, remove loose and flaking coatings, treat or fill defects and make sure the substrate is sound and dry before applying exterior products suited to masonry, render, timber or metal.

Whether it's a front door and windows or a full facade, we plan the work around the forecast and keep the site tidy throughout.

Weather & durability

Preparation and the right coating

Exterior paint fails when it's applied over dirt, damp or loose material, or when the product isn't right for the surface. Peeling and blistering are almost always a preparation problem.

We assess each surface and choose an exterior coating to suit it — breathable masonry finishes for walls, and appropriate primers and topcoats for timber and metal. Painting is scheduled for dry conditions and suitable temperatures so coatings can cure properly.

Things we consider

  • Surface type and existing coating
  • Signs of damp, cracking or blown render
  • Exposure to sun and rain
  • Weather window and drying times
Our process

How we approach exterior work

Methodical preparation comes first — the paint is the final stage.

  1. Survey the exterior

    We check surfaces, access needs and any repairs, and talk through colours and finishes.

  2. Clean and prepare

    Surfaces are washed down, scraped, filled and primed; surroundings are protected.

  3. Apply exterior coatings

    Suitable products applied in the right conditions, allowing proper drying between coats.

  4. Inspect and tidy

    We review the finished work with you and leave the site clean.
