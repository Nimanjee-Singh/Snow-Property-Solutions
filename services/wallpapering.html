Wallpaper hanging

Pattern, texture and character

Wallpaper brings something paint can't — pattern, texture and depth. But it's unforgiving: every seam, bubble and misaligned repeat is on show.

We plan each wall before the first drop is hung: where the pattern will centre, how it will meet corners and openings, and which way the joins will fall in the light. Walls are prepared so the paper sits flat and adheres properly.

You choose the paper; we make sure it looks the way it did in the showroom.

Preparation & setting out

The details that make wallpaper look right

Most wallpaper problems — lifting seams, visible joins, bubbles — come from poor preparation or rushed setting out. Walls need to be sound, smooth and suitably sized so the paper adheres evenly.

Before hanging, we measure and plan where the pattern will start and end, so the design is centred on the focal point and the least visible join falls in a discreet corner.

If your walls need more work first, we can carry out plaster repairs or hang lining paper as part of the job.

Our process

From bare wall to finished paper

Each step is planned to protect the paper and the result.

  1. Assess walls & paper

    We check wall condition and review your chosen paper’s type, repeat and hanging instructions.

  2. Strip and prepare

    Old paper removed, walls filled, sanded and sized, with lining paper where needed.

  3. Set out and hang

    The pattern is centred and each drop hung, matched and smoothed carefully.

  4. Trim and inspect

    Edges trimmed cleanly at ceilings, skirting and fittings; seams checked before sign-off.
