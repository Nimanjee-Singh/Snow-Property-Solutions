Interior painting

A finish that makes the whole room feel new

A well-painted room is calm to look at: even colour, crisp edges, and no shadows of old marks showing through.

We achieve that by spending the right amount of time on preparation. Walls and ceilings are inspected, holes and cracks filled, surfaces sanded and dusted, and stains or bare plaster primed before any topcoat is applied. Woodwork is keyed and cleaned so new paint adheres properly.

We work in occupied homes and empty properties alike, protecting floors and furniture and keeping disruption to a minimum.

Surface preparation

Why preparation matters indoors

Paint highlights whatever is underneath it. Without filling and sanding, small dents, old nail holes and uneven patches become more visible under a fresh coat — especially in side light from windows.

We prepare each surface according to its condition: washing down where there's grease or marks, scraping back loose paint, filling and sanding defects, and spot-priming repairs and stains so the final coats dry evenly.

Where walls need more than filling, our plastering and repairs service gives them a sound base first.

Our process

Four stages, every room

A consistent method so every surface gets the attention it needs.

  1. Assess the surfaces

    We look at the condition of walls, ceilings and woodwork and agree the finishes you want.

  2. Prepare and protect

    Furniture and floors are covered, surfaces filled, sanded and primed, and edges masked.

  3. Apply the selected finishes

    Coats applied evenly in the agreed colours and sheens, with time allowed for proper drying.

  4. Final inspection

    We check every surface in good light, touch up anything needed and walk through the room with you.
