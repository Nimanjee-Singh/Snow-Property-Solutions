Professional Plastering & Wall Repair Services in London

Cracks, holes and damaged plaster repaired properly, so the decoration that follows has a smooth, sound surface to sit on.

Surface repair

The foundation of every good finish

Even the best paint can't hide a cracked ceiling or a patchy wall. Repairing the surface first is what makes the finish look professional.

We repair damaged walls and ceilings as a standalone service or as part of a wider decorating project. That ranges from filling hairline cracks and patching holes to skimming areas where the surface has become rough or uneven.

The goal is simple: a smooth, stable surface that's ready to take paint or paper.

Why it matters

How good preparation supports the finish

Paint and wallpaper follow the shape of the surface underneath. Unrepaired cracks show as lines, patched areas show as rough spots, and fresh plaster that isn't sealed can absorb paint unevenly and leave a blotchy finish.

By repairing and preparing surfaces properly, we give the decoration the best chance of looking even and lasting well. New plaster is left to dry before decorating and then sealed with a suitable mist coat or primer.

Repairs pair naturally with interior painting and wallpapering.

Our process

Repair, refine, ready

Repairs are planned around drying times so decorating can follow on smoothly.

  1. Inspect the damage

    We assess the cracks or damaged areas and explain the repair approach.

  2. Protect and prepare

    Floors and furniture are covered, and loose or failed material is removed.

  3. Repair and smooth

    Areas are filled, patched or skimmed, then sanded smooth once dry.

  4. Seal and check

    Repairs are sealed ready for decoration and checked for a flush, even surface.
